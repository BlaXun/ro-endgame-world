# renewal-world-minibosses

The minibosses of renewal's new lands, for pre-renewal. Needs
[renewal-world](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app/tree/main/registry/mods/renewal-world) and
[renewal-world-monsters](../renewal-world-monsters).

| Where | Minibosses |
|---|---|
| Malangdo culvert (mal_dun01) | Wild Rider |
| Izlude dungeon 5 (iz_dun05) | King Dramoh |
| Scaraba Hole 3 (dic_dun03) | the gold Scarabas (One-Horn, Two-Horn, Antler, Rake), and the eggs they hatch from |

What they summon comes with them. A miniboss is converted like the ordinary
monsters and set at its region's level step; its drops follow the same rules.
The converted equipment and cards a miniboss drops come from
[renewal-world-gear](../renewal-world-gear) and
[renewal-world-cards](../renewal-world-cards) when those are on. The MVPs are
[renewal-world-mvps](../renewal-world-mvps), which needs this mod: the Gold
Queen Scaraba summons the gold Scarabas.

Generated by
[`build_monsters.py`](../../tools/renewal-world-monsters/build_monsters.py),
with renewal-world-monsters; `BOSSES.md` beside it has the method.
