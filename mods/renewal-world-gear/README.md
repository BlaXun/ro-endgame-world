# renewal-world-gear

Renewal's equipment from the monsters of renewal's new lands, converted for
pre-renewal: 54 weapons and armour pieces, and renewal's sets of them at a
third of their bonus. Needs [renewal-world-monsters](../renewal-world-monsters).

Each piece drops from the monster renewal gives it to, at renewal's rate. The
mod does not change the monster tables: `npc/renewal_world_gear_drops.txt`
adds the drops when the server starts (`addmonsterdrop`), to each monster that
is loaded, so a boss's items drop only while
its mod is on
([renewal-world-minibosses](../renewal-world-minibosses) or
[renewal-world-mvps](../renewal-world-mvps)). The server log says how
many it added: `renewal-world-gear: N equipment drops added to M monsters`.

How an item is converted: held to pre-renewal's best of its kind (weapon
attack at most a little above the best pre-renewal weapon of the type),
renewal-only bonuses translated or dropped, single-skill bonuses at a third.
The client's names and descriptions (`System/itemInfo.lua`) describe each item
as it is here, sets included.

Generated by
[`build_monsters.py`](../../tools/renewal-world-monsters/build_monsters.py)
(with `build_equipment.py` and `build_combos.py`); `DROPS.md` and
`SINGLE_SKILL_GEAR.md` beside it have the rules, `equipment.csv` the
overrides. Do not edit the files by hand.
