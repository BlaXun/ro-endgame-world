# renewal-world-monsters

The ordinary monsters of renewal's new lands, for pre-renewal with levels up
to 175. Needs [renewal-world](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app/tree/main/registry/mods/renewal-world) for the maps.

Every open region gets renewal's spawns: the Bifrost fields, Eclage, Dewata,
Malangdo, Malaya, Izlude dungeon 5, El Dicastes, Rockridge and Verus, and,
while the **quest areas** setting is on, some of the areas renewal keeps behind
episode quests (turn it off together with renewal-world's setting of the same
name). Monsters pre-renewal already has keep their pre-renewal stats; the new
ones keep their renewal level and are set at their region's level step,
tested in party fights: a region is party content, a step harder than the one
below.

They drop renewal's loot. Etc and usable items pre-renewal lacks come with
this mod (`db/item_db.yml`, with their client entries in
`System/itemInfo.lua`). The rest is in mods of their own, each optional:

| Mod | What it adds |
|---|---|
| [renewal-world-minibosses](../renewal-world-minibosses) | the minibosses: King Dramoh, Wild Rider, the gold Scarabas and their eggs |
| [renewal-world-mvps](../renewal-world-mvps) | the MVPs and what they summon (needs the minibosses mod) |
| [renewal-world-gear](../renewal-world-gear) | renewal's equipment these monsters drop, converted for pre-renewal |
| [renewal-world-cards](../renewal-world-cards) | their cards, converted for pre-renewal |

Characters reach the levels above 99 with a mod that raises the level cap;
without one the higher regions are simply too strong.

## Where things are defined

Everything here is generated by
[`tools/renewal-world-monsters/build_monsters.py`](../../tools/renewal-world-monsters/build_monsters.py)
from rAthena's renewal tables; one run writes this mod and the three above.
Do not edit the files by hand: change the tables beside the script
(`monster_maps.csv`, `monsters.csv`, `steps.csv`, `bands.csv`) and run

```
python3 tools/renewal-world-monsters/build_monsters.py --rathena ../rathena --write
```

`BALANCING.md` there has the rules and the fight tests behind the numbers.
