# renewal-world-mvps

The MVPs of renewal's new lands, for pre-renewal. Needs
[renewal-world](https://github.com/Flux159/ragnarokoffline.app/tree/main/registry/mods/renewal-world),
[renewal-world-monsters](../renewal-world-monsters) and
[renewal-world-minibosses](../renewal-world-minibosses) (the Gold Queen Scaraba
summons the gold Scarabas).

| Where | MVPs |
|---|---|
| Krakatau volcano (dew_dun01) | Leak |
| Izlude dungeon 5 (iz_dun05) | Kraken |
| Scaraba Hole 2 (dic_dun02) | Scaraba Queen |
| Scaraba Hole 3 (dic_dun03) | Gold Queen Scaraba |
| Rockridge mine (rockmi1) | Spider Chariot |
| Quest areas (setting on) | Boss Pitaya (ba_lost), Dark Lord (gl_chyard_), Nightmare Amon Ra (moc_prydn2), R48-85-Bestia (sp_rudus2) |

The monsters only they summon come with them. A boss is converted like the
ordinary monsters and set at its region's level step, then judged in attrition
fights against a party of that level; its MVP rewards and drops follow the
same rules as the ordinary monsters'. The converted equipment and cards a boss
drops come from [renewal-world-gear](../renewal-world-gear) and
[renewal-world-cards](../renewal-world-cards) when those are on.

**Quest areas** (on by default): the MVPs of the areas renewal keeps behind
episode quests. Turn it off together with renewal-world's setting of the same
name.

Generated by
[`build_monsters.py`](../../tools/renewal-world-monsters/build_monsters.py),
with renewal-world-monsters; `BOSSES.md` beside it has the method and the
fights. Do not edit the files by hand.
